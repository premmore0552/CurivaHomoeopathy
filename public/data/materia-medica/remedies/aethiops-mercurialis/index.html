Aethiops Mercurialis

Black Sulphur Mercury

  • Skin
  • Eyes
  • Glands
  • Relationship
  • Dose

This is a very useful remedy in scrofulous conditions, especially in children. It is indicated in skin diseases, such as eczema, especially of the scalp and face. It is also useful in cases of enlarged glands and chronic inflammation of the eyes.

Skin

Eruptions on the scalp and face, with much itching. Scabby eruptions. Crusta lactea.

Eyes

Chronic inflammation of the eyes and eyelids. Phlyctenular conjunctivitis. Scrofular ophthalmia.

Glands

Enlarged glands in the neck and other parts of the body.

Relationship

Compare: Calcarea carbonica; Mercurius; Sulphur.

Dose

Third to sixth trituration.
