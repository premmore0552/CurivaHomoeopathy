This remedy is characterized by a general feeling of heaviness and congestion, especially in the portal system and the rectum. It is useful in cases of haemorrhoids and chronic diarrhoea. There is a general lack of control over the anal sphincter. The patient is often a large, phlegmatic person. There is a marked tendency to portal congestion.

Mind

Confused. Forgetful. Aversion to mental and physical labor.

Head

Headache, with a sensation of weight in the forehead. Vertigo.

Eyes

Eyes red and burning. Sensation of heat.

Mouth

Metallic taste. Tongue yellow and coated.

Stomach

Appetite poor. Thirst. Heaviness and pressure after eating.

Abdomen

Distended and tender. Rumbling. Flatulence. Sharp pains in the region of the liver.

Rectum

Haemorrhoids, like bunches of grapes; purple, painful, and itching. Anus feels as if it were open. Constant desire for stool. Stool passes involuntarily while urinating or passing gas. Diarrhoea of thin, watery stools, with much gas.

Urinary

Frequent desire to urinate. Scanty urine.

Extremities

Aching and lameness in the limbs. Heaviness in the legs.

Modalities

Worse in the morning and in hot weather. Better from cold applications and in the open air.

Relationship

Compare: Aesculus; Collinsonia; Nux vom.; Sulphur.

Dose

Second to sixth potency.
