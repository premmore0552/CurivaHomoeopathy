Ammonium Valerianicum

Valerianate of Ammonia

  • Mind
  • Head
  • Stomach
  • Relationship
  • Dose

This remedy is characterized by its action on the nervous system. It is especially useful in cases of nervous disorders and hysteria. There is a general feeling of agitation and restlessness. It is also useful in cases of headaches and insomnia in nervous people.

Mind

Nervous and agitated. Restless. Irritable. Insomnia.

Head

Neuralgic pains in the head. Headache, with a sensation of weight in the forehead.

Stomach

Appetite poor. Nausea. Flatulence.

Relationship

Compare: Valeriana; Kali bromatum; Ignatia.

Dose

Third to sixth potency.
