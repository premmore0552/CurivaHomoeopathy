This remedy is characterized by a general lack of vital force and a tendency to decomposition. It is useful in cases of extreme debility, particularly in people with a sedentary lifestyle. There is a general feeling of heaviness and tiredness. The patient is often a stout, elderly person. There is a marked tendency to haemorrhages and a feeling of chilliness.

Mind

Confused and forgetful. Depression and anxiety. Fears death. Aversion to work.

Head

Headache, with a sensation of weight in the forehead. Vertigo on rising. Scalp feels tight.

Eyes

Eyes red and burning. Dimness of vision. Double vision.

Nose

Chronic catarrh of the nose. Dryness and obstruction. Sneezing. Nosebleed.

Face

Expression of pain and anxiety. Pale and sallow. Redness of the face.

Mouth

Dryness of the mouth. Tongue feels dry and sticky. Foul breath. Toothache.

Throat

Dryness and rawness in the throat. Swollen and sore. Sensation of a lump in the throat.

Stomach

Appetite poor. Thirst. Heaviness and pressure after eating. Eructations.

Abdomen

Distended with gas. Rumbling. Flatulence.

Rectum

Constipation. Stool large and hard. Haemorrhoids, with burning and itching.

Urinary

Frequent desire to urinate. Scanty urine.

Female

Menses profuse and early. Leucorrhoea.

Respiratory

Shortness of breath on motion. Violent cough, with a sensation of oppression in the chest. Hoarseness.

Heart

Palpitation, with a sensation of oppression in the region of the heart.

Extremities

Aching and lameness in the limbs. Heaviness in the legs. Trembling of the hands.

Skin

Itching and burning. Dry and scaly. Eruptions like miliary rash.

Modalities

Worse from cold air and in the morning. Better from warmth and in the open air.

Relationship

Compare: Lachesis; Rhus toxicodendron; Arsenicum album.

Dose

Third to thirtieth potency.
