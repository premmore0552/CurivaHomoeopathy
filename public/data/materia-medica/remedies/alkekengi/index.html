Alkekengi

Winter Cherry

  • Stomach
  • Abdomen
  • Urinary
  • Relationship
  • Dose

This remedy is indicated in cases of urinary disorders, especially those related to the bladder and kidneys. It is useful in cases of dropsy and chronic inflammation of the urinary tract. There is a general feeling of soreness and tenderness in the renal region.

Stomach

Appetite poor. Nausea. Burning in the stomach.

Abdomen

Distended and tender. Rumbling.

Urinary

Frequent desire to urinate. Scanty, dark urine. Burning in the urethra. Stones in the bladder.

Relationship

Compare: Berberis; Cantharis; Lycopodium.

Dose

Tincture to third potency.
