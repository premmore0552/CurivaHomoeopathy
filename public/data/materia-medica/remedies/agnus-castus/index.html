The most effective use of Agnus is in cases of sexual debility and loss of sexual power. It is indicated in both sexes for these conditions. There is a general lack of vital force, with coldness of the body. Premature old age.

Mind

Absent-minded. Forgetful. Depression and anxiety. Fears death.

Head

Dull headache, especially in the forehead. Vertigo.

Eyes

Pupils dilated. Dimness of vision.

Stomach

Appetite poor. Nausea. Heaviness in the stomach.

Abdomen

Distended with gas. Rumbling.

Male

Sexual debility. Coldness of the genitals. Yellow discharge from the urethra.

Female

Leucorrhoea, with a sensation of coldness. Loss of sexual desire.

Skin

Itching and burning. Yellow spots on the skin.

Relationship

Compare: Selenium; Phosphorus; Caladium.

Dose

First to sixth potency.
