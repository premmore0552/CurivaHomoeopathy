This remedy is indicated in cases of malignant diseases, especially of the throat. It is useful in cases of low-grade fevers, with extreme debility and a tendency to a typhoid state. The patient is dull, confused, and may be semi-conscious. There is a general foulness of all secretions.

Mind

Dull and confused. Semi-conscious. Anxiety and restlessness.

Head

Dull headache, with a sensation of weight. Vertigo.

Face

Dark red or mahogany color. Expression of pain and anxiety.

Throat

Swollen and dark red. Ulcers on the tonsils. Foul-smelling breath. Hawking of bloody mucus.

Stomach

Appetite poor. Nausea and vomiting.

Abdomen

Tender and distended. Chronic diarrhoea of foul-smelling stools.

Respiratory

Shortness of breath. Cough, with bloody expectoration.

Skin

Dark red or purple eruptions. Petechiae.

Modalities

Worse from motion and in the morning.

Relationship

Compare: Baptisia; Lachesis; Rhus toxicodendron.

Dose

First to sixth potency.
