This remedy is characterized by a general state of mental and physical debility, with a marked loss of memory and a feeling of being divided into two personalities. It is useful in cases of nervous exhaustion and digestive disturbances. The characteristic symptom is a feeling of a plug in various parts of the body and a marked improvement of symptoms from eating.

Mind

Loss of memory. Confused. Irritable. Feeling as if there were two wills, one commanding and the other forbidding. Fears. Aversion to work.

Head

Headache, with a sensation of a plug in the head. Vertigo. Scalp feels tight.

Eyes

Eyes red and burning. Dimness of vision. Sensation of a plug in the eyes.

Ears

Deafness. Tinnitus. Sensation of a plug in the ears.

Nose

Chronic catarrh of the nose. Dryness and obstruction. Sneezing. Sensation of a plug in the nose.

Face

Expression of pain and anxiety. Pale and sallow. Sensation of a cobweb on the face.

Mouth

Dryness of the mouth. Tongue feels dry and sticky. Foul breath.

Throat

Dryness and rawness in the throat. Sensation of a plug in the throat. Difficulty in swallowing.

Stomach

Appetite poor, but symptoms are better while eating and after eating. Nausea. Heaviness and pressure after eating.

Abdomen

Distended with gas. Rumbling. Flatulence. Chronic constipation. Sensation of a plug in the abdomen.

Rectum

Constipation. Stool large and hard. Sensation of a plug in the rectum. Much straining, even with soft stool.

Urinary

Frequent desire to urinate. Scanty urine.

Female

Menses profuse and early. Leucorrhoea.

Respiratory

Shortness of breath on motion. Violent cough, with a sensation of a plug in the larynx.

Back

Stiffness and aching in the back. Sensitiveness of the spine to touch.

Extremities

Aching and lameness in the limbs. Heaviness in the legs. Trembling of the hands. Numbness and tingling.

Skin

Itching and burning. Dry and scaly. Eruptions like miliary rash. Intensely itching eczema.

Modalities

Worse in the morning and from mental labor. Better while eating and after eating.

Relationship

Compare: Rhus toxicodendron; Cypripedium; Chelidonium.

Dose

Third to thirtieth potency.
