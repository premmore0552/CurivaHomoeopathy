Ammonium Benzoicum

Benzoate of Ammonia

  • Head
  • Stomach
  • Abdomen
  • Urinary
  • Relationship
  • Dose

This remedy is indicated in cases of urinary disorders, especially those related to the kidneys. It is useful in cases of dropsy and albuminuria. There is a general feeling of heaviness and soreness in the renal region. It is also useful in cases of gout and rheumatism.

Head

Headache, with a sensation of weight in the forehead. Vertigo.

Stomach

Appetite poor. Nausea. Burning in the stomach.

Abdomen

Distended and tender. Rumbling.

Urinary

Scanty, dark, and foul-smelling urine. Albuminuria. Burning in the urethra. Stones in the kidneys.

Relationship

Compare: Benzoic acid; Berberis; Lycopodium.

Dose

Third to sixth trituration.
