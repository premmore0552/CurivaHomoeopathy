A remedy for acute catarrh, with increased secretions from the nose and eyes. It is indicated in cases of hay-fever and common cold. The characteristic symptom is a profuse, acrid nasal discharge and a profuse, bland lacrimation. There is a general feeling of heat and burning.

Mind

Confused. Forgetful. Fears that the pain will become unbearable.

Head

Headache, especially in the forehead. Vertigo.

Eyes

Profuse, bland lacrimation. Eyes red and burning. Photophobia.

Nose

Profuse, acrid nasal discharge, which excoriates the upper lip and nose. Sneezing. Sensation of a lump in the nose.

Throat

Sensation of a splinter in the throat. Hoarseness. Hawking of mucus.

Stomach

Appetite poor. Thirst. Burning in the stomach.

Abdomen

Distended with gas. Rumbling. Sharp pains.

Respiratory

Violent cough, with a sensation of tearing in the larynx. Hoarseness. Shortness of breath.

Extremities

Aching and lameness in the limbs. Neuralgic pains in the fingers.

Modalities

Worse in a warm room and in the evening. Better in the open air.

Relationship

Compare: Euphrasia; Pulsatilla; Arsenicum album.

Dose

Third to thirtieth potency.
