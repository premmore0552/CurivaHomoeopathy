Agave Americana

Century Plant

  • Relationship
  • Dose

This remedy is indicated in cases of scurvy, with much weakness and debility. It is also useful in cases of dropsy and chronic diarrhoea. There is a general soreness and tenderness of the body.

Mouth

Gums swollen, bleeding, and sore. Tongue pale and flabby.

Stomach

Appetite poor. Nausea. Burning in the stomach.

Abdomen

Distended and tender. Chronic diarrhoea of thin, watery stools.

Skin

Pale and waxen. Petechiae. Scurvy.

Relationship

Compare: Arsenicum; China; Phosphorus.

Dose

Tincture to third potency.
