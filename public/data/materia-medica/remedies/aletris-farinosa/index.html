Aletris Farinosa

Star Grass

  • Mind
  • Stomach
  • Abdomen
  • Female
  • Relationship
  • Dose

This remedy is especially indicated in cases of extreme debility and lack of vital force, particularly in women. It is useful in cases of anaemia and digestive disorders. There is a general feeling of weariness and exhaustion. It is often called 'the china of the uterine organs.'

Mind

Confused and forgetful. Depression and anxiety.

Stomach

Appetite poor. Nausea. Disgust for food. Heaviness in the stomach.

Abdomen

Distended with gas. Flatulence. Chronic constipation.

Female

Uterine debility. Leucorrhoea. Prolapse of the uterus. Habitual miscarriage.

Relationship

Compare: Helonias; China; Hydrastis.

Dose

Tincture to third potency.
