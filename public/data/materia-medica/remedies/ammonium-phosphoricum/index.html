Ammonium Phosphoricum

Phosphate of Ammonia

  • Relationship

This remedy is indicated in cases of metabolic disorders and respiratory issues. It is useful in cases of gout and rheumatism, especially when there is a tendency to deposits in the joints. There is a general feeling of weakness and debility. It is also useful in cases of chronic bronchitis.

Head

Headache, with a sensation of weight in the forehead. Vertigo.

Stomach

Appetite poor. Nausea. Heaviness and pressure after eating.

Urinary

Frequent desire to urinate. Scanty, dark urine. Deposits in the urine.

Respiratory

Shortness of breath on motion. Violent cough, with a sensation of oppression in the chest.

Extremities

Aching and lameness in the limbs. Pain and swelling in the joints. Gouty deposits.

Relationship

Compare: Ammonium carbonicum; Lithium carbonicum; Guaiacum.

Dose

Third to sixth potency.
