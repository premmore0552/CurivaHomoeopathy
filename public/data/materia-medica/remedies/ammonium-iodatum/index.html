Ammonium Iodatum

Iodide of Ammonia

This remedy is characterized by its action on the glandular system and the respiratory organs. It is useful in cases of enlarged glands and chronic inflammation of the mucous membranes. There is a general feeling of weakness and debility. It is also useful in cases of chronic bronchitis and syphilis.

Head

Headache, with a sensation of weight in the forehead. Vertigo.

Eyes

Eyes red and burning. Dimness of vision.

Nose

Chronic catarrh of the nose. Dryness and obstruction. Sneezing.

Throat

Irritation and rawness in the throat. Hawking of mucus. Cough. Enlarged tonsils.

Stomach

Appetite poor. Nausea. Heaviness and pressure after eating.

Respiratory

Shortness of breath on motion. Violent cough, with a sensation of oppression in the chest. Hoarseness.

Glands

Enlarged and hard glands in the neck and other parts of the body.

Relationship

Compare: Iodine; Kali iodatum; Calcarea iodata.

Dose

Third to sixth potency.
