Ammonium Bromatum

Bromide of Ammonia

This remedy is characterized by its action on the respiratory organs and the nervous system. It is useful in cases of chronic catarrh and chronic cough. There is a general feeling of irritation and burning. It is also useful in cases of nervous disorders, such as epilepsy and hysteria.

Head

Headache, with a sensation of weight in the forehead. Vertigo.

Eyes

Eyes red and burning. Itching of the eyelids.

Nose

Chronic catarrh of the nose. Dryness and obstruction. Sneezing.

Throat

Irritation and rawness in the throat. Hawking of mucus. Cough. Sensation of a lump in the throat.

Stomach

Appetite poor. Nausea. Burning in the stomach.

Abdomen

Distended and tender. Flatulence.

Respiratory

Shortness of breath. Wheezing. Violent cough, with a sensation of oppression in the chest. Hoarseness.

Relationship

Compare: Kali bromatum; Bromium; Hyoscyamus.

Dose

First to third trituration.
