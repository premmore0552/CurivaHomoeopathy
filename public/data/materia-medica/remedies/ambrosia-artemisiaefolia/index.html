Ambrosia Artemisiaefolia

Rag-weed

  • Relationship

This remedy is characterized by its marked action on the mucous membranes of the nose and eyes. It is especially indicated in cases of hay-fever and common cold. There is a general feeling of irritation and burning. The characteristic symptom is a profuse, watery nasal discharge and a profuse lacrimation.

Eyes

Profuse lacrimation. Eyes red and burning. Itching of the eyelids.

Nose

Profuse, watery nasal discharge. Sneezing. Stuffed feeling in the nose. Nosebleed.

Throat

Irritation and rawness in the throat. Hawking of mucus. Cough.

Respiratory

Shortness of breath. Wheezing. Violent cough, with a sensation of oppression in the chest.

Abdomen

Distended and tender. Flatulence. Chronic diarrhoea.

Relationship

Compare: Allium cepa; Euphrasia; Sabadilla.

Dose

Tincture to third potency.
