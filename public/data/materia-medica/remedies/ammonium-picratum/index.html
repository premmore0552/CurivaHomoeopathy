Ammonium Picratum

Picrate of Ammonia

  • Head
  • Stomach
  • Urinary
  • Relationship
  • Dose

This remedy is indicated in cases of nervous disorders and fevers. It is useful in cases of malarial conditions and neuralgic pains. There is a general feeling of weakness and debility. It is also useful in cases of headaches and mental fatigue.

Head

Headache, with a sensation of weight in the forehead. Vertigo. Neuralgic pains in the head.

Stomach

Appetite poor. Nausea. Heaviness in the stomach.

Urinary

Frequent desire to urinate. Scanty urine.

Relationship

Compare: Picric acid; China; Arsenicum album.

Dose

Third to sixth trituration.
