Medicago Sativa - California Clover

This remedy is a great fat producer and is indicated in cases of malnutrition and lack of appetite. It is useful in cases of anaemia and general debility. It increases the quantity and quality of milk in nursing mothers. There is a general feeling of well-being and improved digestion.

Mind

Clear and bright. Improved memory. Depression and anxiety.

Head

Headache, with a sensation of weight in the occiput. Vertigo.

Stomach

Increased appetite. Thirst. Improved digestion.

Abdomen

Distended with gas. Flatulence.

Urinary

Increased quantity of urine. Increased urea.

Relationship

Compare: China; Avena sativa; Hydrastis.

Dose

Five to ten drops of the tincture.
