This remedy is indicated in cases of digestive and respiratory disorders. It is useful in cases of dyspepsia and chronic cough. The patient is often a great meat-eater. There is a general feeling of heaviness and fullness.

Mind

Confused and forgetful. Depression.

Head

Headache, with a sensation of weight in the forehead. Vertigo.

Mouth

Profuse salivation. Tongue pale and flabby. Garlic-like breath.

Throat

Sensation of a hair in the throat. Swollen and sore.

Stomach

Increased appetite. Thirst. Heaviness and pressure after eating. Burning eructations.

Abdomen

Distended with gas. Flatulence. Sharp pains in the region of the liver.

Rectum

Diarrhoea of thin, watery stools. Prolapse of the rectum.

Respiratory

Violent cough, with bloody expectoration. Shortness of breath on motion.

Relationship

Compare: Capsicum; Arsenicum album; Senega.

Dose

Third to sixth potency.
