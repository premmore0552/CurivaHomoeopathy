This remedy is characterized by a general nervous hypersensitivity and a feeling of embarrassment and shyness. It is useful in cases of nervous disorders and digestive disturbances in elderly people. There is a general feeling of weakness and lack of vital force. The patient is often a thin, nervous person. There is a marked tendency to premature old age.

Mind

Shy and embarrassed. Aversion to the presence of others. Confused and forgetful. Loquacity. Anxiety.

Head

Headache, with a sensation of weight in the forehead. Vertigo on rising. Scalp feels tight.

Eyes

Eyes red and burning. Dimness of vision.

Ears

Deafness. Tinnitus.

Mouth

Dryness of the mouth. Tongue feels dry and sticky. Foul breath.

Stomach

Appetite poor. Thirst. Heaviness and pressure after eating. Eructations.

Abdomen

Distended with gas. Rumbling. Flatulence.

Rectum

Constipation. Stool large and hard. Frequent desire for stool.

Urinary

Frequent desire to urinate. Scanty urine.

Respiratory

Shortness of breath on motion. Violent cough, ending in eructations. Hoarseness.

Heart

Palpitation, with a sensation of oppression in the region of the heart.

Sleep

Restless. Sleeplessness from nervousness.

Extremities

Twitching and jerking of the muscles. Trembling of the hands. Numbness and tingling.

Skin

Itching and burning. Dry and scaly.

Modalities

Worse in the presence of others and in the morning. Better from open air and cold food.

Relationship

Compare: Moschus; Phosphorus; Valeriana.

Dose

Third to thirtieth potency.
