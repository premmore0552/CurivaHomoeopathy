This remedy is characterized by a general tendency to hardening and contraction of the tissues. It is useful in cases of constipation and chronic inflammation of the mucous membranes. There is a general feeling of dryness and lack of secretions. It is also useful in cases of malignant diseases and tumors.

Mind

Confused and forgetful. Depression and anxiety.

Head

Headache, with a sensation of weight in the forehead. Vertigo.

Throat

Dryness and rawness in the throat. Swollen and hard. Sensation of a splinter in the throat.

Stomach

Appetite poor. Thirst. Heaviness and pressure after eating.

Abdomen

Distended and hard. Flatulence. Chronic constipation.

Rectum

Stool large, hard, and dry. Much straining. Constipation. Haemorrhoids, with burning and itching.

Female

Hardening of the uterus. Leucorrhoea.

Respiratory

Shortness of breath on motion. Violent cough, with a sensation of tearing in the larynx.

Relationship

Compare: Alumina; Baryta carbonica; Conium.

Dose

Third to thirtieth potency.
