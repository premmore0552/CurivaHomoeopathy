Alstonia Scholaris

Dita Bark

  • Stomach
  • Abdomen
  • Stool
  • Relationship
  • Dose

This remedy is indicated in cases of malarial conditions and digestive disorders. It is useful in cases of chronic diarrhoea and dysentery. There is a general feeling of weakness and debility. It is also useful in cases of low-grade fevers.

Stomach

Appetite poor. Nausea. Heaviness in the stomach.

Abdomen

Distended and tender. Chronic diarrhoea of thin, watery stools. Dysentery.

Stool

Thin, watery, and foul-smelling. Bloody mucus.

Relationship

Compare: China; Arsenicum album; Podophyllum.

Dose

Tincture to third potency.
