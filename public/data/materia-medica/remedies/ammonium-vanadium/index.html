Ammonium Vanadium

Vanadiate of Ammonia

  • Head
  • Stomach
  • Respiratory
  • Extremities
  • Relationship
  • Dose

This remedy is characterized by its action on the metabolic processes and the respiratory organs. It is useful in cases of chronic wasting diseases and chronic respiratory disorders. There is a general feeling of weakness and debility. It is also useful in cases of anemia and malnutrition.

Head

Headache, with a sensation of weight in the forehead. Vertigo.

Stomach

Appetite poor. Nausea. Heaviness and pressure after eating.

Respiratory

Shortness of breath on motion. Violent cough, with a sensation of oppression in the chest.

Extremities

Aching and lameness in the limbs. Heaviness in the legs.

Relationship

Compare: Vanadium; Phosphorus; Arsenicum album.

Dose

Third to sixth potency.
