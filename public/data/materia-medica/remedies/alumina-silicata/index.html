This remedy is indicated in cases of respiratory and digestive disorders. It is useful in cases of chronic catarrh and chronic inflammation of the mucous membranes. There is a general feeling of weakness and debility. It is also useful in cases of skin diseases.

Head

Headache, with a sensation of weight in the forehead. Vertigo.

Nose

Chronic catarrh of the nose. Dryness and obstruction. Sneezing.

Stomach

Appetite poor. Nausea. Heaviness and pressure after eating.

Abdomen

Distended with gas. Flatulence. Chronic constipation.

Respiratory

Shortness of breath on motion. Violent cough, ending in sneezing. Hoarseness.

Skin

Dry and scaly. Itching and burning.

Relationship

Compare: Alumina; Silicea; Calcarea carbonica.

Dose

Third to thirtieth potency.
