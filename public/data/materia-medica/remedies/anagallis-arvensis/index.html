Anagallis Arvensis

Scarlet Pimpernel

  • Mind
  • Skin
  • Extremities
  • Relationship
  • Dose

This remedy is characterized by its action on the skin and the mind. It is useful in cases of skin diseases and mental disturbances. There is a general feeling of irritation and burning. It is also useful in cases of enlarged glands and gout.

Mind

Confused and forgetful. Loquacity. Irritable.

Skin

Intense itching and burning. Eruptions like miliary rash. Eczema on the fingers and hands. Warts on the hands.

Extremities

Aching and lameness in the limbs. Gouty pains in the joints.

Relationship

Compare: Rhus toxicodendron; Anacardium; Selenium.

Dose

Third to thirtieth potency.
