This remedy has a marked action on the nervous system, producing a variety of symptoms, such as twitching, jerking, and trembling. It is especially indicated in cases of chorea and other nervous disorders. It is also useful in skin diseases, such as chilblains, with much itching and burning. There is a general sensitiveness to cold air.

Mind

Singing, chatting, but does not answer. Delirium. Loquacity. Fearless.

Head

Vertigo from sunlight, and on walking. Headache, as if from a nail driven into the head. Icy coldness on the head.

Eyes

Twitching of the eyelids and eyeballs. Double vision. Dimness of vision.

Face

Twitching of the facial muscles. Redness and burning of the face.

Stomach

Great appetite. Burning in the stomach. Empty eructations.

Abdomen

Distended with gas. Rumbling. Sharp pains in the region of the liver.

Rectum

Diarrhoea of thin, yellow-greenish mucus. Itching and burning of the anus.

Urinary

Frequent desire to urinate. Scanty urine.

Respiratory

Shortness of breath on motion. Violent cough, ending in sneezing.

Heart

Palpitation, with a sensation of oppression in the region of the heart.

Back

Stiffness and aching in the back. Sensitiveness of the spine to touch.

Extremities

Twitching and jerking of the muscles of the limbs. Trembling of the hands. Chilblains, with much itching and burning.

Skin

Chilblains. Itching and burning as if frozen. Eruptions like miliary rash.

Modalities

Worse from cold air and after eating. Better from gentle motion.

Relationship

Compare: Cimicifuga; Calcarea carbonica; Ignatia; Tarentula.

Dose

Third to thirtieth potency.
