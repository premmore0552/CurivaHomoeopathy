This remedy is characterized by its action on the respiratory organs and the eyes. It is useful in cases of chronic catarrh and chronic inflammation of the mucous membranes. There is a general feeling of weakness and debility. The patient is often an elderly person with chronic respiratory issues.

Eyes

Eyes red and burning. Dimness of vision. Sensation of a film over the eyes.

Nose

Chronic catarrh of the nose. Dryness and obstruction.

Throat

Irritation and rawness in the throat. Hawking of mucus. Cough.

Stomach

Appetite poor. Nausea. Heaviness in the stomach.

Respiratory

Shortness of breath on motion. Violent cough, with a profuse expectoration of mucus. Hoarseness. Chronic bronchitis.

Extremities

Aching and lameness in the limbs. Heaviness in the legs.

Relationship

Compare: Senega; Antimonium tartaricum; Ipecacuanha.

Dose

Third to sixth potency.
