Ammonium Causticum

This remedy is characterized by its powerful action on the mucous membranes, producing intense irritation and burning. It is especially indicated in cases of acute respiratory disorders and severe inflammation of the throat. There is a general feeling of suffocation and a marked tendency to cardiac and respiratory failure.

Throat

Intense burning and pain in the throat. Swollen and dark red. Ulcers on the tonsils. Sensation of a lump in the throat. Difficulty in swallowing and breathing.

Respiratory

Violent cough, with a sensation of tearing in the larynx. Shortness of breath. Wheezing. Sensation of suffocation. Hoarseness.

Stomach

Intense burning in the stomach. Nausea and vomiting of bloody matter.

Abdomen

Tender and distended. Flatulence.

Heart

Palpitation, with a sensation of oppression in the region of the heart. Weak and rapid pulse.

Relationship

Compare: Arsenicum album; Cantharis; Phosphorus.

Dose

Third to sixth potency.
