This remedy is characterized by its action on the mucous membranes and the liver. It is useful in cases of chronic catarrh and chronic inflammation of the respiratory organs. There is a general feeling of heaviness and soreness in the liver region. The patient is often a stout person with a sedentary lifestyle. There is a marked tendency to portal congestion and a feeling of chilliness.

Mind

Confused and forgetful. Depression and anxiety. Fears death. Aversion to work.

Head

Headache, with a sensation of weight in the forehead. Vertigo on rising. Scalp feels tight.

Eyes

Eyes red and burning. Dimness of vision. Sensation of a film over the eyes.

Nose

Chronic catarrh of the nose. Dryness and obstruction. Sneezing. Nosebleed.

Face

Expression of pain and anxiety. Pale and sallow. Sensation of a cobweb on the face.

Mouth

Dryness of the mouth. Tongue feels dry and sticky. Foul breath.

Throat

Dryness and rawness in the throat. Swollen and sore. Sensation of a lump in the throat. Hawking of mucus.

Stomach

Appetite poor. Thirst. Heaviness and pressure after eating. Eructations.

Abdomen

Distended with gas. Rumbling. Flatulence. Sharp pains in the region of the liver.

Rectum

Constipation. Stool large and hard. Haemorrhoids, with burning and itching. Anus feels sore and raw.

Urinary

Frequent desire to urinate. Scanty urine.

Female

Menses profuse and early. Leucorrhoea.

Respiratory

Shortness of breath on motion. Violent cough, with a sensation of oppression in the chest. Hoarseness.

Back

Stiffness and aching in the back. Sensitiveness of the spine to touch.

Extremities

Aching and lameness in the limbs. Heaviness in the legs. Trembling of the hands. Numbness and tingling.

Skin

Itching and burning. Dry and scaly. Eruptions like miliary rash.

Modalities

Worse in the morning and from cold air. Better from open air and moderate motion.

Relationship

Compare: Ammonium carbonicum; Lachesis; Rhus toxicodendron.

Dose

Third to thirtieth potency.
