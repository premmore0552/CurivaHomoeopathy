Agraphis Nutans

Bluebell

  • Nose
  • Throat
  • Ears
  • Relationship
  • Dose

This is a very useful remedy in cases of catarrhal conditions, especially of the nose and throat. It is indicated in children with enlarged tonsils and adenoids. There is a general tendency to take cold easily.

Nose

Chronic catarrh of the nose. Obstruction of the nose. Sneezing.

Throat

Enlarged tonsils and adenoids. Throat feels swollen and sore. Hawking of mucus.

Ears

Deafness from catarrh of the Eustachian tube. Otorrhoea.

Relationship

Compare: Hydrastis; Calcarea phosphorica; Silicea.

Dose

Third to sixth potency.
