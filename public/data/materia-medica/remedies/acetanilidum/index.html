Acetanilidum

Antifebrinum

  • Head
  • Eyes
  • Heart
  • Relationship
  • Dose

Increased susceptibility to cold. Depresses heart, respiration and blood pressure, lowers temperature. Cyanosis and collapse.

Head

Enlarged sensation. Fainting. Moral depravity.

Eyes

Pallor of optic discs, contracted visual field and shrinking retinal vessel mydriasis.

Heart

Weak, irregular, with blue mucous membranes, albuminuria, oedema of feet and ankles.

Relationship

Compare: Antipyrin.

Dose

Used as a sedative and antipyretic for various forms of headache and neuralgia in doses of one to three grains. For the homoeopathic indications use the third potency.
