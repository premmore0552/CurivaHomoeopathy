Amygdalus Persica

Peach Tree

  • Stomach
  • Abdomen
  • Relationship
  • Dose

This remedy is characterized by its action on the digestive system, especially the stomach. It is useful in cases of nausea and vomiting, particularly that related to pregnancy. There is a general feeling of irritation and burning in the stomach. It is also useful in cases of morning sickness.

Stomach

Intense nausea and vomiting. Persistent vomiting. Morning sickness of pregnancy. Irritation and burning in the stomach. Thirst.

Abdomen

Distended and tender. Flatulence.

Relationship

Compare: Ipecacuanha; Symphoricarpus; Nux vomica.

Dose

Tincture to third potency.
