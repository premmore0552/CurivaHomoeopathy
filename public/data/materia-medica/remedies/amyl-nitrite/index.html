This remedy is characterized by its powerful action on the circulatory system, producing rapid and intense flushing and heat. It is especially indicated in cases of angina pectoris and flushing during menopause. There is a general feeling of fullness and throbbing in the head and heart. The characteristic symptom is a sudden flushing of the face and upper part of the body.

Head

Intense flushing of the face and head. Throbbing headache. Sensation of fullness and pressure. Vertigo.

Eyes

Eyes red and bloodshot. Dimness of vision. Sensation of heat.

Face

Sudden flushing, becoming deep red or purple. Sensation of heat.

Throat

Sensation of constriction in the throat. Difficulty in swallowing.

Respiratory

Shortness of breath. Wheezing. Sensation of suffocation. Rapid and deep breathing.

Heart

Violent palpitation. Throbbing in the region of the heart. Angina pectoris. Rapid and full pulse.

Female

Flushing during menopause. Profuse sweating.

Modalities

Worse from motion and in a warm room. Better from open air and cold applications.

Relationship

Compare: Glonoinum; Lachesis; Belladonna.

Dose

Third to sixth potency. Also by inhalation of a few drops.
