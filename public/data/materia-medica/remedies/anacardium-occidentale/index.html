Anacardium Occidentale

Cashew Nut

  • Skin
  • Stomach
  • Abdomen
  • Relationship
  • Dose

This remedy is characterized by its action on the skin and the digestive system. It is useful in cases of skin diseases and chronic diarrhoea. There is a general feeling of weakness and debility. It is also useful in cases of enlarged glands.

Skin

Eruptions, with much itching and burning. Erysipelas. Impetigo.

Stomach

Appetite poor. Nausea. Heaviness and pressure after eating.

Abdomen

Distended and tender. Flatulence. Chronic diarrhoea.

Relationship

Compare: Anacardium orientale; Rhus toxicodendron; Cantharis.

Dose

Third to sixth potency.
