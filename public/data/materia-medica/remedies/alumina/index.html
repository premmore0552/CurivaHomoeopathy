This remedy is characterized by a general sluggishness and lack of vital force. It is useful in cases of constipation and chronic inflammation of the mucous membranes. There is a general feeling of dryness and lack of secretions. The patient is often a thin, dry person. There is a marked tendency to paralysis and loss of control over the muscles.

Mind

Confused and forgetful. Depression and anxiety. Fears that the mind will give way. Time passes too slowly.

Head

Headache, with a sensation of weight in the forehead. Vertigo. Scalp feels tight.

Eyes

Burning and itching of the eyelids. Dimness of vision. Double vision.

Nose

Chronic catarrh of the nose. Dryness and obstruction. Sneezing.

Face

Expression of pain and anxiety. Pale and sallow. Sensation of a cobweb on the face.

Mouth

Dryness of the mouth. Tongue feels dry and sticky.

Throat

Dryness and rawness in the throat. Sensation of a splinter in the throat. Constant desire to swallow. Hawking of mucus.

Stomach

Appetite poor. Thirst. Heaviness and pressure after eating. Craving for dry food.

Abdomen

Distended with gas. Rumbling. Flatulence. Chronic constipation.

Rectum

Stool large, hard, and dry. Much straining, even with soft stool. Constipation. Anus feels dry and sore.

Urinary

Frequent desire to urinate. Scanty urine.

Female

Leucorrhoea, with a sensation of dryness. Menses scanty and delayed.

Respiratory

Shortness of breath on motion. Violent cough, ending in sneezing.

Back

Stiffness and aching in the back. Sensitiveness of the spine to touch.

Extremities

Aching and lameness in the limbs. Heaviness in the legs. Trembling of the hands.

Skin

Dry and scaly. Itching and burning. Eruptions like miliary rash.

Modalities

Worse in the morning and from dry food. Better from open air and moderate motion.

Relationship

Compare: Alumen; Baryta carbonica; Conium; Silicea.

Dose

Third to thirtieth potency.
