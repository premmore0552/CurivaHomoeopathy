The characteristic symptoms are related to the brain and the nervous system, with gastro-intestinal disturbance. It is especially indicated in children during dentition and in summer complaints. The child is unable to digest milk, which is vomited in large, sour curds. Great weakness and sleepiness after vomiting or stool. Indigestion of teething children. Inability to think, to fix the attention. Idiocy in children.

Mind

Inability to think, to fix the attention. Confused. Anxiety and restlessness. Hallucinations. Fears death.

Head

Headache, with a sensation as if the head were in a vice. Vertigo, with sleepiness. Weight in the occiput.

Eyes

Pupils dilated. Eyes turned downward. Photophobia.

Face

Expression of great anxiety and pain. Linea nasalis marked. Pale face.

Mouth

Dryness of the mouth. Aphthae. Tongue feels as if too long.

Stomach

Intolerance of milk; it is vomited in large, sour curds, or as soon as swallowed. Regurgitation of food an hour after eating. Violent vomiting of white, frothy matter. Great thirst.

Abdomen

Distended and tense. Coldness of the abdomen.

Rectum

Diarrhoea of thin, yellow-greenish, or bloody mucus. Tenesmus. Much weakness and sleepiness after stool.

Modalities

Worse in the morning and from milk. Better in the open air.

Relationship

Compare: Antimon. crud.; Cicuta; Calcarea carbonica.

Dose

Third to thirtieth potency.
