The action of this drug is most marked on the lower bowel, producing engorged haemorrhoidal veins, with characteristic backache, with absence of constipation. Much pain after stool, with burning and prickling. Heart and sacro-iliac joints also seem to be affected. The characteristic backache of Aesculus is in the region of the sacro-iliac joints, which feel as if they would give way. Aesculus is especially indicated in the stasis of the portal system. It has also a marked action on the respiratory organs. In cases of chronic catarrh of the nose, with a sensation of dryness and rawness in the throat.

Head

Dull frontal headache, from right to left, with flying pains in the epigastrium.

Nose

Dryness of posterior nares and throat, with sneezing and coryza. Sensation of a sting in the nose. Stinging and burning in the nose.

Mouth

Scalded feeling on the tongue. Salivation. Metallic taste.

Throat

Dryness and rawness in the throat, with a sensation as if a splinter were lodged in the throat. Congested and swollen. Constant desire to swallow. Hawking of mucus.

Stomach

Burning in the stomach. Pressure as from a stone.

Abdomen

Fullness and tenderness in the region of the liver. Throbbing in the abdomen.

Rectum

Dry, aching. Feels full of small sticks. Anus raw, sore. Much pain after stool, with burning and prickling. Haemorrhoids, like ground nuts, purple; painful, burning, and itching; rarely bleeding. Rectum feels as if it would fall out. Stool large, dry, hard, difficult. Constipation.

Urinary

Frequent desire to urinate. Scanty, dark urine.

Female

Leucorrhoea, with lameness of the back. Throbbing in the pelvis.

Respiratory

Dry, hacking cough, with hoarseness. Shortness of breath on motion.

Heart

Palpitation, with a sensation of heaviness in the region of the heart.

Back

Lameness and weakness in the back. Pain in the region of the sacro-iliac joints, as if they would give way. Backache, extending to the hips.

Extremities

Aching and lameness in the limbs. Hands feel as if they were swollen.

Modalities

Worse in the morning and on motion. Better in the open air.

Relationship

Compare: Aloe; Collinsonia; Nux vom.; Sulphur.

Dose

Second to third potency.
