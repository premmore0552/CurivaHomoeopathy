Alnus Serrulata

Red Alder

  • Skin
  • Glands
  • Female
  • Relationship
  • Dose

This remedy is indicated in cases of skin diseases and glandular disorders. It is useful in cases of eczema and chronic inflammation of the mucous membranes. There is a general tendency to enlarged glands and a feeling of debility.

Skin

Eczema, with much itching and burning. Scabby eruptions. Impetigo.

Glands

Enlarged glands in the neck and other parts of the body.

Female

Leucorrhoea. Amenorrhoea.

Relationship

Compare: Calcarea carbonica; Mercurius; Sulphur.

Dose

Tincture to third potency.
